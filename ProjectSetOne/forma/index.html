The studio

FORMA is fourteen architects who believe a building should feel inevitable — as if the site had been waiting for it.

We work slowly and in section. Every project begins with the path of the sun, the grain of the ground and the material within a day's walk. What follows is not decoration but consequence: walls placed to hold an afternoon shadow, apertures cut for a single view.

The result is architecture that is quiet at first glance and inexhaustible at second — buildings people keep discovering, and keep.
