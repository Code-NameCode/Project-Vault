Good to know

Frequently asked

How long do carpets take to dry?

Typically 1–3 hours with our low-moisture hot-water extraction. Good ventilation can bring it under 2 hours.

Are your products safe for kids and pets?

Yes - we use plant-based, non-toxic, residue-free products certified safe for children and animals.

Do you guarantee stain removal?

We succeed on 97% of stains. If a treated stain returns or we can't remove it, that room is free - simple as that.

Do I need to move furniture?

No - our technicians move light and medium furniture as part of the service. Just clear small valuables beforehand.

What areas do you serve?

All metro suburbs within a 25-mile radius. Enter your postcode at booking and we'll confirm instantly.
