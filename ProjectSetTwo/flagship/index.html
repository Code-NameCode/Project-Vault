03 — How We Work

The Meridian Method.

Six weeks to truth

Every engagement begins with a forensic discovery: architecture audits, dependency mapping, risk quantification and a cost-of-inaction model. We tell you what is actually true — including when the honest answer is "don't build it."

  • Full-stack technical due diligence
  • Risk & dependency heatmaps
  • Business-case modelling with named owners

Architecture as a contract

We design for the decade, not the demo. Reference architectures are stress-tested against failure, cost and compliance scenarios, then signed as an engineering contract — so delivery has nowhere to hide.

  • Failure-mode testing on every design
  • Five-year total-cost modelling
  • Compliance built into the blueprint

Shipped, not slideware

Senior-led delivery pods ship in two-week increments with live dashboards your executives can read. No surprise milestones, no zombie programmes — 94% of our engagements land on the original business case.

  • Executive-visible delivery dashboards
  • Two-week verified increments
  • Named accountable delivery lead

The long tail is the point

Most firms leave at go-live. We take pager duty. Follow-the-sun operations hubs in London, Singapore and Austin keep the systems we build running at 99.99% — and feed every incident back into better engineering.

  • Three global NOC/SOC hubs
  • 99.99% contractual availability
  • Continuous improvement loop
